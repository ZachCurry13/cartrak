# Insights

**More, Insights** (also on the Reports page) turns your driving and charging history into something worth looking at. Everything
is worked out on your own server from what CarTrak already stores; nothing is sent anywhere.

## Highlights

- **Wrapped**: a month or a year as full-screen slides you tap through, like a story. Tap the right side for the next slide, the
  left side to go back. The slides are your distance (compared with something everyone knows, such as Route 66 end to end), your
  car's **personality**, the energy you charged and what it cost (and saved against a gas car when one is set under Charging
  settings), where every kWh went, the towns you visited and the places you went most, highlights such as the longest and coldest
  drive, achievements earned, and a typical day. The last slide is an **infographic**: one tall picture of the month, which
  **Share picture** sends to another app or saves to the phone. On the first of each month, Home shows "Your September is ready"
  for a week, and phones that turned on the **Your month, wrapped** alert are told.
- **Everywhere you've been**: every route you drove, drawn faint on a dark map and added on top of each other, so the roads you
  drive most glow brightest. It opens on where most of your driving is; zoom out for the long trips. Routes come from phone GPS,
  the road estimate or the car's own positions, as on the Trips map.
- **Achievements**: badges with a ring that fills as you get closer, such as 1,000 miles, a 1,000-mile month, Road trip, a 30-day
  streak, Hypermiler, Sub-zero, the 150 kW club, Night owl, 1 MWh charged and Explorer (25 towns). Each shows when it was earned.
  A new one appears in the Inbox, and on phones that turned on the **Achievement earned** alert. The first check after an update
  only notes what you already have, so a long history does not arrive as a burst of old news.
- **Where every kWh went**: the energy charged at home and in public, split into city driving, highway driving (an average of 70
  km/h or more), climate and keeping the car awake while parked, what was lost between the charger and the battery, and what is
  still in the battery.
- **A day in the life**: a 24-hour clock of when the car usually drives (outer ring) and charges (inner ring), with the share of
  time it is parked and when it usually leaves on a weekday and plugs in at night.

## The charts

The tabs under Highlights hold the detailed charts, each for the last 90 days, 12 months or everything:

- **Charging**: totals, energy by month (home and public), what a kWh cost by month, where the energy came from, when you plug in
  (hour of the week) and your fastest charges.
- **Driving**: efficiency against the outside temperature with your own trend line, a calendar of distance per day, efficiency by
  average speed, how long your trips are and when you drive.
- **Records**: a recap card for any month (with **Play it as Wrapped**) and your personal bests, each opening its trip.
- **Battery**: the battery level day by day, the share of time spent at each level, remote starts (how many, the energy they took and how long the car ran), usable capacity and the 12 V battery.

Tap a bar, a square or a dot on any chart to read it; drag along a line chart.

## Personalities

A personality is picked from how the car was used in the month or year: **The Night Charger** (most charging started after 9 pm),
**The Fast-Charge Fan** (much of the energy from fast chargers), **The Commuter** (weekday rush hours), **The Road Tripper** (much of
the distance on drives of 50 miles or more), **The Weekend Wanderer**, **The Local Hero** (mostly short trips) and **The Hypermiler**.
The one the car fits best wins, and the runner-up is named too.

## Tips from your charging

The Charging page can show **Tips from your charging**, worked out from the last 60 days. Each appears only when the numbers clearly
back it up, and the card is not shown at all when there is nothing worth saying:

- **Charging to 100% most nights**: most home charges went to 95% or more, but the driving after them used little of the battery.
- **What the night rate would save**: when a cheaper night rate is set under Charging settings, the energy charged at home outside
  those hours, priced at the difference.
- **A fast charger that is slow for you**: a place where your fast charges averaged well under your usual fast-charging speed.
- **Under 10% at the charger**: when that happened three times or more.
- **What public charging costs on top**: when public charging cost at least twice your home price per kWh.
