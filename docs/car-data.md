# Car data

The **Data** tab shows everything the car tells CarTrak, in plain words and sorted into tabs. It is the place to look when you want to know "what is that number?".

| Tab | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Summary** | The few things most people want (state, charge, range, energy left, odometer) and how fresh the data is |
| **Battery and charging** | The main battery (charge on the dash and the true charge, energy, capacity, voltage, temperature), the 12 V battery, and what the charger is doing. Gas and hybrid cars also get fuel and engine |
| **Driving** | Controls (gear, ignition, speed, pedals), motion sensors, direction, and energy recovered by braking |
| **Body and security** | Locks, doors, windows, seat belts, alarm and remote start, and each tire |
| **Settings and schedules** | Software updates, departure times and other settings, read only |
| **Car** | The model and what Ford shares about this car and the connection (never the VIN or location) |
| **All fields** | Every raw value exactly as Ford sent it, with search. It is a large download, so Data saver asks first |

A tab shows only what your car reports, so a car without a value simply has no row for it.

Tap the **?** next to any value to see what it means and Ford's own name for it. Ford sends some values only when the car wakes, so a value that is much older than the reading says how old it is.

## Field watcher
Open **Field watcher** from the Car data page to see every kind of reading the car sends, over the last day, two days or week: its latest value, how often it changes and when it last did. A tag says what CarTrak uses it for, whether it is only shown on the Car data page, or not used at all. A reading that never changes (the oil life of an electric car) is listed too. The readings are thinned to about 400 so the page stays quick on a long window.

## Looking back in time
CarTrak keeps every reading, so the page can show the car as it was at any earlier moment:
- **Earlier** and **Later** step between readings.
- Pick a **day and time** and tap **Show the car at this time**. It shows the last reading CarTrak had by then.
- The chart of the day shows the battery percentage, with blue bands for driving and green for charging. Tap it to jump to that moment.
- **Latest** returns to now.

Nothing is deleted as it ages, so this works for as long as CarTrak has been running. Readings from before an update that added a value may not have that value.
